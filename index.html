Bear Market Protocol
🐻 Down 10–20%
Normal correction. Keep DCA unchanged. Consider adding 10–25% extra to core. This is expected — do not pause step-up schedule.
📉 Down 20–40%
Bear market. Double monthly DCA if cash flow allows. Shift asymmetric toward core (VOO, VTI, QQQ). Hold all positions — do not sell.
💥 Down 40%+
Market crash. Deploy any reserves above 6-month emergency fund. Maximize TFSA contribution room. This is a generational buying opportunity.
🔄 Recovery Phase
Never sell during recovery to "lock in gains." Rebalance back to phase allocation annually. VOO and QQQ have always reached new highs.
