Bear Market Protocol
Down 10-20%
Normal correction. Keep DCA unchanged. Consider adding 10-25% extra to core. Do not pause step-up schedule.
Down 20-40%
Bear market. Double monthly DCA if cash flow allows. Shift asymmetric toward core (VOO, VTI, QQQ). Hold all positions.
Down 40%+
Market crash. Deploy reserves above 6-month emergency fund. Maximize TFSA contribution room. Generational buying opportunity.
Recovery Phase
Never sell during recovery. Rebalance back to phase allocation annually. VOO and QQQ have always reached new highs.
Wealthsimple · Tax Optimization
1
FHSA First (if eligible) — $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime. Tax deduction on contribution AND tax-free withdrawal for first home. Double benefit no other account offers. Use for growth ETFs (QQQ, VOO). If you own a home or are ineligible, skip to TFSA.
2
TFSA Second — $7,000/year (2025+). All gains completely tax-free — capital gains, dividends, everything. Hold QQQ, SOXX, SCHG here for maximum tax-free growth. Lifetime room ~$95K if eligible since 2009.
3
RRSP for US Dividends — Canada-US tax treaty: US dividends inside RRSP avoid the 15% withholding tax. Put SCHD, VYM, JEPI here specifically. RRSP contribution room = 18% of earned income, up to ~$32K/yr.
4
USD Account — $10/mo — All 10 ETFs trade in USD. Wealthsimple USD account eliminates 1.5% FX spread. On $500/mo USD buys = $90/yr saved. Waived automatically at $100K+ assets. Pays for itself in Month 1.
5
Enable DRIP — Turn on Dividend Reinvestment in Wealthsimple settings. SCHD and VYM dividends auto-reinvest into more shares. Keep DRIP on until Year 20+. Never manually withdraw dividends early.
6
Step-Up Calendar — Update recurring auto-deposit every 4 months: Jan 1, May 1, Sep 1 each year for 15 years. After Year 15, final amount is locked permanently — no more updates needed.
Core Principles
A
Step-Up DCA is Your Greatest Weapon — 10% increase every 4 months means contributions grow exponentially while early volatility is irrelevant. Even modest step-ups dramatically change 30-year outcomes vs flat DCA.
B
Reinvest All Dividends Until Year 20+ — DRIP during accumulation and compounding phases. SCHD/VYM dividends reinvested for 20 years become a meaningful compounding multiplier.
C
Annual Rebalancing — January — Rebalance once per year inside TFSA/RRSP (no tax consequences). Trim winners back to phase allocation targets. Add to laggards.
D
Variable SWR — This model uses 4% SWR for horizons up to 30 years, and a more conservative 3.5% for 35+ year horizons. Based on Morningstar 2026 research showing 3.5% has a 90%+ success rate over 40+ years.
